# Project Governance

Restaurant Intelligence is currently maintained by the repository owner.

## Decision making

Changes are evaluated against four priorities:

1. factual reliability and source transparency;
2. usefulness for restaurant and food-service decisions;
3. reproducibility of calculations and data handling;
4. maintainability and responsible data use.

## Contributions

External contributions are welcome through issues and pull requests. A contribution may be declined when its source cannot be verified, its assumptions are unclear, it introduces sensitive data, or its maintenance cost is disproportionate to its value.

## Roadmap

Roadmap items are proposals until implemented and validated. Documentation should distinguish current capability from planned capability.
