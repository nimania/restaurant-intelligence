# Publishing policy

Food Industry Intelligence is designed as an indexing and intelligence layer, not an article-republication system.

## What may be published

- Publisher/source name
- Original publisher URL
- Publication timestamp
- Original headline as supplied by the source/feed
- Short feed-provided description when available
- Industry categories and topic tags
- Detected brand/entity labels
- Relevance score
- Aggregate trend counts and acceleration metrics
- Persian interface labels for categories, brands and aggregate signals

## What is not published

- Full publisher article bodies
- Machine-translated publisher headlines
- Machine-translated publisher summaries
- Persian paraphrases that substitute for the original article
- Large copied excerpts assembled from the publisher page

## Persian-language policy

Persian is used for navigation, taxonomy and intelligence metadata. The system may say that a topic or brand is trending, how often it appears, or what category it belongs to. It must not present a translated or paraphrased version of publisher article text as a replacement for reading the source.

## Source-first reading

Each article card should link directly to the original publisher. The source remains the canonical place to read the article.

## Feed descriptions

Short descriptions supplied directly through RSS/Atom may be retained as source-provided metadata. They remain in the source language and should be displayed conservatively rather than expanded into a substitute for the article.
